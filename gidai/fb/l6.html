Lesson 6

Supply Drop — the future's second thing. "A big job gets easy when you cut it into small parts."
▶ OPEN THE SLIDES — FB-P6-draft3

AT A GLANCE — THE 1-MINUTE VERSION

🎯 WHAT THIS LESSON WANTS FROM YOU: 

teach one word — DECOMPOSE (cut a big job into small parts) · bigger kids also: SUBPROBLEM (one small part) · 

one technique — say the parts first, then code one part at a time and fly it before adding the next · 

one belief — "Any big job gets easy when I cut it into small parts." If the kids leave with those three, you won.


"After today I can break a big job into small parts."  (the Promise — Slide 4, checked on Slide 17)


WHAT THE KID WALKS OUT WITH: A supply plane their crew coded part by part: drop at A, drop at B, fly home.

WHAT THE PARENT HEARS AT HOME: "We built a supply plane and learned to cut a big job into small parts — like baking a cake: eggs, mix, bake, decorate."

✅ SUCCESS: within 1–2 weeks the kid splits a chore into parts out loud before starting it  ·  ❌ FAIL: at Slide 17 nobody can say why cutting the job into parts helped

⏱ LATE? THE 60-SECOND PREP:– 🔴 Hubs, Double Motors and Motors charged. Build and fly the supply plane once yourself (C1, page 32).– 🔴 Bigger kids: code.legoeducation.com → PIN 1304. Younglings: NEW project → Icon blocks — practise the 3 parts in icons once.– Per crew: its own table, one tablet, paper circles for zones A, B (C), a few bricks for a tree.– Game (slide 16): nothing to print — four pictures on the slide.

  • "Our plane is on the map. Look — ONE new spot." ❓ "What do builders need before they can start?"
SLIDE 3Message from the future: supplies90: 2 · 60: 2 min
  • "Before we build anything, we need supplies: food, snacks, juice. No roads, nowhere to land. Our plane is a SUPPLY plane!"
  • ❓ "How do we deliver with nowhere to land?" → drop it from the sky.
SLIDE 4Promise90: 2 · 60: 1 min
  • "After today I can break a big job into small parts." Checked on slide 17.
SLIDE 5Rules90: 1 · 60: 1 min
  • Quick recall. New: "When a crew talks, everyone listens."
SLIDE 6One big job = 3 small parts90: 1 · 60: 1 min
  • "Tidying your room is a BIG job. Cut it into 3: toys, books, clothes. Easy!"
SLIDE 7Our big job = 3 parts90: 1 · 60: 1 min
  • "Our big job: drop the supplies. FLY THERE · DROP · FLY HOME."
SLIDE 8Word of the day: DECOMPOSE90: 1 · 60: 1 min
  • "Cutting a big job into parts is called DECOMPOSE." Everyone: "Part 1 is… Part 2 is… Part 3 is…"
  • Bigger kids: one part = a SUBPROBLEM.
SLIDE 9Today's plan90: 1 · 60: 1 min
  • Build → code in 3 parts → deliver → game. Running late? The game gets shorter, never the build.
SLIDE 10Big build: the supply plane90: 10 · 60: 12 min
  • Same plane as last week + one more motor that drops the box. C1, page 32. Finished early? Help another crew.
SLIDE 11Predict + Try it90: 11 · 60: 10 min
  • Predict (tablets down): plane on START, read the 3 parts, put ONE minifigure where the box will land.
  • Try it: connect both motors, run the code. Did it land on the minifigure?
  • Bigger kids: PIN 1304.
  • ⚠️ YOUNGLINGS: ICON BLOCKS — NOT THE PIN.
    NEW project → Icon blocks. Same 3 parts in icons: forward (fly there) · Motor (drop) · turn + forward (fly home). Build it on the projector; crews copy.
SLIDE 12Plan your delivery90: 5 · 60: 3 min
  • How it plays out (5 min):
  • 1 Each crew clears its own table.
  • 2 Builder sets up: START at the table edge · a small tree of bricks in the middle · two paper circles A and B beyond the tree, a hand-width apart.
  • 3 The crew points and says the parts out loud: "Part 1 drop at A · Part 2 drop at B · Part 3 fly home." Navigator writes them on 3 sticky notes and lays them in order next to the tablet.
  • 4 Check one kid per crew: ❓ "Which part is first?" Done = every kid can say the 3 parts.
  • Younglings: the mentor puts down zone A only; the crew says 2 parts (fly + drop · fly home).
SLIDE 13Part by part90: 20 · 60: 10 min
  • Pairs: pair 1 = blue + red · pair 2 = green + yellow. One tablet; it changes hands each round.
  • Round 1 (~6 min): pair 1 codes Part 1 (fly to A + drop). Pair 2 puts the plane on START, loads the supply, watches: hit or miss? Miss → pair 1 changes only Part 1.
  • Round 2 (~6 min): the tablet goes to pair 2. They add Part 2 under Part 1 (fly to B + drop). Pair 1 resets the plane and reloads. Fly from START: A, then B. Miss at B → fix only Part 2.
  • Round 3 (~5 min): the tablet goes back to pair 1. Add Part 3 (fly home). Fly the whole delivery. Done = a supply on A and on B, plane back at START.
  • If it misses: "Fix only the newest part." Finished early (bigger kids) → slide 14.
  • Younglings: zone A only, 2 rounds (Round 1 fly + drop · Round 2 fly home). The mentor sits with one crew at a time.
  • ⚠️ YOUNGLINGS: ICON BLOCKS — NOT THE PIN.
    NEW project → Icon blocks. Same 3 parts in icons: forward (fly there) · Motor (drop) · turn + forward (fly home). Build it on the projector; crews copy.
SLIDE 14Add zone C 🟡 BIGGER KIDS ONLY90: 8 · 60: skip min
  • Zone C = Part 4. The next pair adds it, then fly it all.
SLIDE 15Show us90: 6 · 60: 2 min
  • Each crew says its parts, then flies. Everyone listens.
  • Younglings: mentor goes crew to crew instead.
SLIDE 16Game: Break It Down90: 12 · 60: 6 min
  • One picture at a time: "Big job! What do you do first? Then? Then?"
  • Cake: eggs · mix · bake · decorate · Snowman: big ball · middle · head · face · Dog: wet · soap · rinse · dry · Flower: dig · seed · soil · water.
  • Bigger kids: ❓ "Which part could a robot do?"
SLIDE 17Promise check90: 1 · 60: 1 min
  • "TRUE?" Thumbs. One kid says why parts help.
SLIDE 18Tomorrow Map: supplies90: 1 · 60: 1 min
  • "Our second thing on the map: supplies! But who will do the work…?" Don't answer.
SLIDE 19Every brick goes home90: 5 · 60: 5 min
  • Clean up in 3 parts: sort · close the box · high five. Charge hubs and motors.
