AT A GLANCE — THE 1-MINUTE VERSION

🎯 WHAT THIS LESSON WANTS FROM YOU: 

teach one word — genuinely · 

one technique — ask a real question, listen, and write the answer down · 

one belief — “The way to make a friend is to be genuinely interested in them.” If the kids leave with those three, you won.


“After today you’ll know the ONE thing that turns a stranger into a friend.”  (the Promise — Slide 6)


WHAT THE KID WALKS OUT WITH: The Friendship Paper — what they knew about a classmate before VR, three real answers after it, and the rule on top.

WHAT THE PARENT HEARS AT HOME: “Can I ask you something I’ve never asked you?”

✅ SUCCESS: within 1–2 weeks a kid asks someone a real question and remembers the answer  ·  ❌ FAIL: the answer boxes on the paper stay empty — nobody actually listened.

⏱ LATE? THE 60-SECOND PREP:– Papers printed (box below) and pens on the tables.– Pair list written: each Viber with the classmate they know least.– Box-breathing app open (brainclub.space) · video on Slide 9 ready.

🖨 PRINT BEFORE CLASS — ONE FRIENDSHIP PAPER PER KID (+2 SPARES)

A5, black and white. The lesson ends on it.

Slide-by-slide

cues, not a word-for-word read · “quoted” = say aloud · ❓ = leading question
SLIDE 1Welcome Back Vibers1 min
  • “Welcome back, Vibers! Today is AItor’s first lesson as our mentor.”
SLIDE 2The Viber Oath3 min
  • Run it exactly as always — everyone standing: 🧠 ONE finger to temple · ❤️ TWO fingers to heart · 💪 THREE fingers to guts. “I am a VIBER. Let’s go!” Never edited.
SLIDE 3The Viber Agreement1 min
  • Read the seven rules fast and firm, grouped by oath line (🧠 1–2 · ❤️ 3–5 · 💪 6–7). Don’t discuss — it’s a contract, not a conversation.
SLIDE 4Last week — Neuron needs a friend2 min
  • “Last week we met AItor, and we know now that he is a tool, not a friend, which is a shame because Neuron has been pretty nervous about this looming danger of Brain Rot, so he could really use a friend. What should we do about this nervousness?”
  • ❓ “Do we know any tool that will help us with that?” → box breathing, from Lesson 3.
SLIDE 5Box Breathing3 min
  • Open the Box Breathing App (brainclub.space → Emotional Regulation Tools). Four rounds: in 4 · hold 4 · out 4 · hold 4. Count the first round out loud, then let the cube lead.
SLIDE 6The Promise — AItor says it1 min
  • “It is true that AItor cannot be your friend, but he can be a mentor in finding friends. He’s read a bunch of books about friendship, and he’s CERTAIN that AFTER TODAY you’ll know the ONE thing that turns a stranger into a friend.”
SLIDE 7What makes people friends?5 min
  • ❓ “What do you think this ONE thing is?” Open discussion. Guide it towards the second question: ❓ “What do people like?”
  • When someone says “everyone likes to talk about themselves”: “YES! And that is the key to making a friend!” → click: BEING GENUINELY INTERESTED IN OTHER PEOPLE.
SLIDE 8Why though?3 min
  • Read the slide. Land the last line: “But it has to be GENUINE. Brains can tell.”
SLIDE 9Video3 min
  • Play it.
SLIDE 10Today’s tool1 min
  • Let them guess, then click: “COMMUNICATING — and we’ll practise it in VR, in Flying Squirrel Chase.”
SLIDE 11Pairs + writing #14 min
  • Pairs: each Viber with the classmate they know least (your list).
  • ⚠ 1 PAIR PER PLANET. Every pair gets its own private lobby. Write pairs + planets on the board.
  • ⚠ FUNDAMENTAL — WRITING #1. Distribute the printed papers. They write what they already know about their partner in the box “What I already know about my partner”. 3 min.
SLIDE 12The questions4 min
  • Read the three questions aloud. Nobody answers yet.
  • 1 · “If you could wake up with a new skill, what would it be?” · 2 · “What would a perfect day look like for you?” · 3 · “What are you proud of that most people don’t know about?”
  • Casting + lobbies (red box). Straps: back first, top last.
SLIDE 13Flying Squirrel Chase20 min
  • Read one question every 3 min, loud, and write it on the board. Asker asks, partner answers, swap.
  • ⚠ Never past 25 min in VR.
SLIDE 14Writing #2 — the answers8 min
  • ⚠ FUNDAMENTAL — WRITING #2. Partner’s answers in boxes 1–3.
  • ⚠ YELLOW QUESTION = PARAMOUNT. Everyone writes their own answer in the TOP box — not in an answer box.
SLIDE 15What did we do to our brain?4 min
  • Prefrontal cortex — “Your brain’s boss. It said: not yet, ask first.”
  • Temporal lobe — “Your understanding station. It turned your partner’s words into meaning.”
  • Striatum — “Your brain’s yum button — like pizza or music. That’s why being asked feels good.”
SLIDE 16🎉 Artefact unlocked — the Friendship Paper2 min
  • They fill the blank at the bottom of the paper themselves → GENUINE.
  • “Today this is merely a piece of paper with some info about one person, but it marks the beginning of a GENUINE connection. This paper may increase in value over time.” The paper goes home.
SLIDE 17Vibe Check3 min
  • Runs via the AI agent. One brave volunteer. Identical every lesson.
  • Homework: “Ask one adult at home a question you’ve never asked them. Keep your Friendship Paper.”
AFTERAfter the lesson — 3 minuteson your phone
  • Phone only. Neither of these works properly on a laptop or the classroom computer.
  • Name box: type L5 then anything you like. We kindly ask not to use your or a kid's real name no matter what the agent tells you.
  • 1 · Talk to the mentor agent — around 2 minutes, you report how the lesson went.
  • 2 · Summon the kids' agent — around 90 seconds with one junior volunteer.
  • Your feedback becomes part of a report the product team reviews every two weeks. Important things — VR kits not working, applications crashing — are flagged to them right away.
